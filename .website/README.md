Full Description:

Overview
Rainbow Unicorn: Color Chain is a small, fast, and colorful vertical shooter. You play as a rainbow unicorn flying through a starry night, firing a continuous stream of rainbow bullets. Enemies come in seven colors. Match your bullet color to an enemy to trigger a chain explosion that wipes out every enemy of the same color on screen. Even mismatched shots still deal damage, so you never get stuck.

Gameplay

Your unicorn fires automatically; you just focus on movement and positioning.

Bullets cycle through the seven rainbow colors (Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, Violet).

The horn of your unicorn glows with the color of your next shot, so you can plan ahead.

Moving leaves a rainbow trail behind you that damages any enemy it touches.

Kill enemies to build up your combo meter. Higher combos mean higher scores and a brighter rainbow background.

Three powerful skills are available on cooldown:

Q — Nova: Fires a 360° burst of 36 rainbow bullets.

E — Power: Doubles your fire rate and bullet count for 6 seconds.

R — Slow: Slows down all enemies and enemy bullets for 5 seconds.

Controls

Move: WASD / Arrow Keys / Mouse (click the canvas to lock the mouse pointer, then move the mouse) / Touch drag on mobile.

Skills: Q = Nova, E = Power, R = Slow.

Start / Restart: Click, tap, or press Space / Enter.

Unlock mouse: Press Esc.

Features

Color-matching chain explosion system.

Rainbow trail that damages enemies.

Three tactical skills with cooldowns.

Mouse lock for precise control.

Dynamic difficulty that scales with your score.

Local high score saved in your browser.

Runs entirely in a single HTML file, no external assets.
